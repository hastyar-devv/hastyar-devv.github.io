01 — About

Obsessed with the details nobody notices —
until they're missing.

I'm Hastyar, a frontend engineer who lives in the gap between design and engineering. I've spent the last five years shipping production React applications for startups that needed to move fast without breaking things.

My stack is JavaScript, React and TypeScript — but the real work is everything around it: state machines that don't lie, bundle sizes that stay small, animations that run at 60fps, and accessibility that isn't an afterthought.

I care about the 16 milliseconds between frames, the keyboard user who can't reach your modal, and the person on a 3G connection in a country you didn't think about. That's the job.
